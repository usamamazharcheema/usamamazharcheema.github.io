Case Study: Customer Health Score Pipeline

Architected an end-to-end customer data platform integrating Airflow orchestration for Salesforce CRM and Pendo product analytics extraction, Onehouse lakehouse ingestion with Apache Hudi, and dbt transformation layers to calculate comprehensive customer health scores across multiple product platforms, with automated daily sync back to Salesforce for customer success operations.

Conductor GmbH

Industry: SaaS / Marketing Technology

Size: 200+

Website: conductor.com

Project Date: July 2025

Conductor is a leading enterprise SEO and content marketing platform serving Fortune 500 companies. This project involved building a data transformation pipeline to aggregate product usage data from multiple platforms, calculate customer health scores across 30/90/180/360-day windows, and sync results to Salesforce for customer success team visibility and proactive account management.

Key Requirements

  • Provide comprehensive customer health visibility by tracking usage across both Conductor Intelligence (CI) and Web Monitoring (CWM) platforms with feature-level granularity
  • Enable trend analysis through rolling window metrics (30, 90, 180, and 360 days) to identify engagement patterns and early warning signals for churn risk
  • Calculate customer health scores using a custom algorithm that combines active users, engagement frequency, time in platform, and feature adoption rates
  • Integrate seamlessly with existing systems—pulling customer data from Salesforce CRM and product analytics from Pendo, then syncing health scores back to Salesforce for Customer Success team access
  • Maintain data accuracy by excluding trial, demo, and PLG accounts from production health calculations while integrating with Catalyst framework for platform health categorization
  • Ensure scalability and future flexibility with database-agnostic design supporting potential platform migrations
  • Deliver real-time insights with daily health score refreshes and monthly snapshot records providing both current metrics and historical trend data

Project Overview

The Goal: Build an automated system to track and predict customer retention risk by analyzing product usage patterns and engagement trends. The platform helps Conductor's teams identify at-risk customers early and take proactive action to prevent churn, while also spotting high-engagement accounts ready for expansion.

The Solution: The system provides a comprehensive view of customer health by tracking usage across Conductor's multiple platforms (Intelligence and Web Monitoring), monitoring engagement with specific features, and analyzing patterns over different time windows (30, 90, 180, and 360 days). It combines customer information from Salesforce CRM with product usage data from Pendo analytics to calculate health scores based on metrics like active users, active days, time spent in platform, and feature adoption rates.

Technically, this is implemented as a full-stack customer data platform (CDP) that orchestrates data flow through four key stages: extraction (Apache Airflow pulling data from Salesforce and Pendo APIs), lakehouse storage (AWS Onehouse with Apache Hudi providing ACID-compliant data lake), transformation (dbt models applying business logic and health scoring algorithms), and operational sync (AWS AppFlow pushing results back to Salesforce for Customer Success team visibility).

The dbt transformation layer implements a medallion architecture with 15+ intermediate models progressively enriching data from staging through to the final Salesforce-ready mart. Key transformations include:

  • Customer filtering: Excludes trial/demo/PLG accounts using CDC config data
  • Temporal aggregations: Rolls up 360 days of usage into MTD, 30d, 90d, 180d, and 360d averages
  • Health score calculation: Custom formula combining active users, active days, and time in platform
  • Cross-platform analytics: Tracks CI, CWM, and combined usage independently
  • Feature-level insights: Granular tracking of Writing Assistant, Content Guidance, Pages, Workspaces
  • Catalyst integration: Applies external health categorization framework (Red/Yellow/Green)

The Challenge

Conductor's customer data was split between Salesforce CRM (customer records, account details, business units) and Pendo product analytics (usage events, visitor tracking, feature adoption, page views). The business needed a unified customer 360-degree view to power health scoring, but faced significant technical and operational hurdles integrating these two critical data sources.

Data Integration Challenges: Building resilient extractors for both platforms with different characteristics— Salesforce's REST API required change data capture via SystemModstamp to track incremental updates across 10+ objects (Account, Contact, Business_Units__c, RecordType, etc.), handling compound field exclusions and deleted record queries. Pendo's aggregation API needed hourly polling for event streams with 4 different aggregation types (configuration, aggregated_configuration, ungrouped, grouped), complex pagination for large result sets, and period-based time windowing. Both required OAuth authentication, retry logic for transient failures, and rate limit handling.

Data Platform Challenges: The system needed to run on AWS Athena for querying the Onehouse data lake, but the team wanted database-agnostic transformation logic to maintain flexibility for potential future migrations. This required abstracting database-specific SQL functions (string operations, date math, aggregations) into reusable macros that could adapt to different query engines. The lakehouse architecture (Onehouse with Apache Hudi) needed stream capture configuration for each table with proper partitioning and merge strategies. Linking Salesforce Account IDs with Pendo Account IDs required complex joining logic, and schema drift from either API could break downstream pipelines.

Business Logic Challenges: Customer health scoring required complex temporal aggregations (MTD, L30D, 3/6/12-month averages) across multiple products (CI and CWM tracked via Pendo) with feature-level granularity (Writing Assistant drafts, Content Guidance usage, Pages engagement). The system had to exclude trial/demo/PLG accounts from production scoring, integrate with the existing Catalyst scoring framework, handle late-arriving data corrections, and sync 100+ calculated fields back to Salesforce daily with upsert logic to avoid duplicate records.

The Approach & Solution

I architected a full-stack customer data platform with four distinct layers, each optimized for its specific role in the data pipeline—from extraction to operational delivery.

1. Extraction & Orchestration (Apache Airflow)
  • Dynamic Pipeline Generation: Built automated DAG creation from configuration files, allowing new data sources to be added without code changes
  • Resilient API Integration: Implemented custom extractors for Salesforce and Pendo with built-in retry logic, rate limiting, and error handling to ensure reliable data collection
  • Incremental Loading: Designed change data capture for Salesforce and event streaming for Pendo, minimizing API calls and data transfer while keeping information current
  • Monitoring & Alerts: Integrated Slack notifications for pipeline failures and data quality issues, enabling quick response to operational problems
2. Lakehouse Storage (AWS Onehouse + Athena)
  • Modern Data Lake: Leveraged Apache Hudi for ACID transactions and time-travel capabilities, ensuring data consistency and enabling historical analysis
  • Schema Evolution: Configured automatic schema adaptation to handle API changes without breaking downstream processes
  • Query Performance: Optimized partitioning and file formats for fast analytical queries via AWS Athena, supporting both batch and ad-hoc analysis
3. Data Transformation (dbt)
  • Modular Design: Built 15+ reusable transformation models organized in layers (staging → business logic → marts), promoting code reuse and maintainability
  • Business Logic Implementation:
    • Linked Salesforce customer accounts with Pendo product usage data
    • Calculated rolling window metrics (30/90/180/360 days) for trend analysis
    • Aggregated usage across CI and CWM products with feature-level granularity
    • Applied proprietary health scoring algorithm combining user engagement, activity frequency, and feature adoption
    • Integrated with Catalyst framework for platform health categorization
  • Database Portability: Built database-agnostic macro layer abstracting SQL functions to support future database migrations, while currently running on AWS Athena for production workloads
  • Quality Assurance: Implemented comprehensive testing with data fixtures and automated validation in CI/CD pipeline
4. Operational Delivery (AWS AppFlow)
  • Salesforce Integration: Configured automated sync of 100+ calculated health score fields back to Salesforce Customer Health Score objects
  • Sync Cadence & Logic:
    • Monthly Record Creation: New Customer Health Score record created for each active account on the first day of every month, providing a monthly health snapshot
    • Daily Data Refresh: All fields on the monthly record updated daily to reflect current rolling averages rather than static month-end values, ensuring real-time accuracy
    • Time Stamp Logic: At month end, Time in Platform fields are time-stamped with the final 30-day calculation and stop collecting new data for that record
    • Rolling Averages Approach: Uses "last X days" calculations instead of calendar months to provide more accurate real-time metrics aligned with the rolling 3-month measurement framework
  • Smart Updates: Implemented upsert logic to update existing records and create new ones, avoiding duplicates while maintaining data history

Business Impact & Results

Customer Retention Improvements: The automated health scoring system became a critical tool for Conductor's customer retention strategy. By providing early warning signals when customer engagement declined, the Customer Success team could intervene proactively—often before customers even realized they weren't maximizing platform value. This predictive approach enabled targeted outreach, personalized training sessions, and strategic check-ins that significantly reduced churn risk.

Cross-Functional Enablement: The health score data transformed decision-making across multiple departments. Sales representatives identified high-engagement accounts for upsell conversations, prioritizing expansion opportunities with the strongest product-market fit. Product managers analyzed feature adoption patterns to inform roadmap priorities, focusing development efforts on capabilities that correlated with high health scores. Executive leadership gained a real-time, data-driven view of customer satisfaction trends, portfolio health, and retention risks across the entire customer base.

Operational Efficiency: By automating health calculations and Salesforce integration, the pipeline eliminated hundreds of hours of manual data analysis previously performed by the CS team. The daily refresh cycle ensured teams always worked with current data, and the comprehensive 100+ field dataset provided unprecedented granularity—tracking not just overall health but product-specific usage (CI vs CWM), feature-level engagement, and temporal trends across multiple time windows.

Strategic Value: The platform's architecture positioned Conductor for future growth. The modular design allows rapid integration of additional data sources, and the database-agnostic dbt macro layer provides migration flexibility if the team ever needs to move from Athena to alternative query engines. The documented business logic creates an organizational knowledge asset that transcends individual team members. Most critically, the system established a data-driven culture where customer health metrics inform every strategic conversation about retention, growth, and product development.
