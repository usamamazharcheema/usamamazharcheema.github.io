Case Study: User Trial Activity & PQL Scoring Pipeline

Engineered a comprehensive trial user analytics sync that tracks product adoption across free trial accounts, calculates Product Qualified Lead (PQL) scores using multi-factor engagement algorithms, and syncs behavioral insights to Salesforce for sales qualification—integrating Pendo product analytics, Zendesk support interactions, and custom dbt transformation logic to identify high-intent trial users ready for sales conversion.

Conductor GmbH

Industry: SaaS / Marketing Technology

Size: 200+

Website: conductor.com

Project Date: Jan 2025 - Feb 2025

Conductor offers a 14-day free trial of their enterprise SEO platform, generating hundreds of trial signups monthly. This project built an automated system to track trial user behavior, calculate lead quality scores based on product engagement patterns, and provide sales teams with data-driven insights to prioritize outreach and improve trial-to-paid conversion rates.

Key Requirements

  • Track comprehensive trial user behavior including feature adoption (page groups, keyword tracking, workspaces), integration setup (Google Search Console, Analytics), and engagement patterns (active hours, explorer searches, content creation)
  • Calculate Product Qualified Lead (PQL) scores using a weighted algorithm that evaluates 15+ behavioral signals including hand-raise events, teammate invitations, support interactions, and platform usage depth
  • Link trial activity data across multiple systems—matching Pendo visitor IDs with Salesforce contacts, connecting Searchlight internal account IDs with trial account identifiers, and correlating Zendesk support tickets with user engagement
  • Identify conversion indicators like trial hand-raise events (demo requests), pricing page inquiries, and in-app conversion attempts to prioritize sales follow-up
  • Sync trial activity metrics and PQL scores to Salesforce User Trial Activity objects, enabling sales reps to view engagement history and lead quality directly in their CRM workflow
  • Track trial lifecycle status (active, expired, converted) with key dates (initiated, user created, end date, first/last login) for accurate funnel analysis and conversion attribution
  • Maintain data accuracy by handling trial source attribution (organic, PLG, marketing campaigns), excluding internal test accounts, and managing late-arriving data from various API sources

Project Overview

The Goal: Build an intelligent trial qualification system that automatically identifies which free trial users show genuine purchase intent based on their product usage patterns. The platform helps Conductor's sales team focus their limited time on the most promising leads rather than manually reviewing hundreds of trial signups, while also providing insights into which features and behaviors correlate with successful conversions.

The Solution: The system captures granular behavioral data from Pendo (product analytics), Zendesk (support engagement), and Salesforce (trial account records), then applies a custom PQL scoring algorithm that weighs 15+ engagement factors. High-value actions like inviting teammates (9 points), integrating Google Search Console (20 points), or connecting analytics platforms (30 points) signal serious evaluation intent. Lower-friction activities like creating workspaces (1 point per workspace) or tracking keywords (1 point per 10 keywords) demonstrate feature adoption. The algorithm caps individual factor contributions to prevent score manipulation while accumulating evidence of genuine product-market fit.

Technically, this is implemented as a multi-stage dbt transformation pipeline with 12 intermediate models that progressively enrich trial data—starting from raw Pendo events, aggregating usage metrics, calculating individual PQL factor scores, applying business logic for hand-raise detection and conversion tracking, then formatting the final dataset for Salesforce sync. The database-agnostic dbt architecture runs on AWS Athena querying the Onehouse data lake, with custom macros abstracting SQL functions to maintain portability across potential database migrations.

The pipeline processes trial user behavior across 12 intermediate dbt models: starting with user trial status tracking (trial dates, source attribution, conversion state), calculating individual PQL factors (hand-raise events, page groups, teammates, keyword tracking, integrations, support engagement, usage metrics), aggregating Pendo events (explorer searches, workspace activity, content creation), linking Salesforce contact records, and formatting the final output with weighted PQL scores (0-500+ scale) and human-readable factor explanations for sales context.

The Challenge

Conductor's sales team faced a critical lead qualification problem: with hundreds of trial signups monthly, representatives couldn't manually review each trial user's activity to identify serious buyers. Trial users who simply logged in once differed fundamentally from those setting up integrations, inviting teammates, and actively exploring features—but this behavioral intelligence was scattered across Pendo analytics, Zendesk support tickets, and Salesforce CRM records with no unified scoring system to prioritize sales outreach.

Data Integration Challenges: Building the qualification system required linking trial user identities across three disparate platforms with different ID schemes. Pendo tracked users by visitor IDs and account IDs, Salesforce stored contacts with 18-character IDs and custom SL User ID fields, while internal systems used Searchlight account identifiers. The pipeline needed to resolve these mappings accurately to attribute behavior correctly, handle users appearing in one system but not others (partial trial completions), and manage the temporal complexity of trials starting, expiring, and converting at different times across systems.

Behavioral Signal Complexity: Product Qualified Lead scoring required translating raw event data into meaningful engagement signals. Pendo captured granular events (page views, button clicks, feature usage) that needed aggregation into business-relevant metrics. The system had to distinguish between different types of engagement—creating a workspace (low friction, 1 point) versus integrating Google Search Console (high intent, 20 points)—and apply appropriate weights reflecting conversion correlation. Certain actions needed ceiling caps (max 40 points for page groups, max 90 points for support interactions) to prevent score manipulation while still rewarding genuine power usage.

Temporal and State Management Challenges: Trial lifecycle tracking introduced complex temporal logic. The system needed to capture trial initiation dates, user creation timestamps, end dates, first/last login times, and hand-raise event dates—all from different source systems with potential clock skew. Trial status (active, expired, converted) changed over time, requiring logic to determine current state. Conversion attribution (trial hand-raise, pricing inquiry, in-app conversion) needed tracking across multiple touch points, and the system had to handle trials extending beyond initial periods, users rejoining after trial expiration, and enterprise deals closing months after trial end.

Sales Enablement Requirements: Beyond just calculating scores, the system needed to provide actionable context for sales reps. High PQL scores alone didn't explain why a lead qualified—was it integration setup? Teammate invitations? Feature depth? The pipeline needed to surface individual factor contributions and present them in Salesforce in formats sales teams could quickly understand during prospecting calls. Additionally, the system had to sync 30+ behavioral metrics (active days, active hours, explorer searches, workspace activity, etc.) alongside PQL scores to enable conversation starters and personalized outreach based on specific product usage patterns.

The Approach & Solution

I architected a multi-stage dbt transformation pipeline with 12 intermediate models that progressively build trial user intelligence—starting from raw event data, through identity resolution and behavioral aggregation, to final PQL scoring and Salesforce formatting. The modular design allows independent testing and modification of each transformation stage while maintaining end-to-end data lineage.

1. Trial Status Foundation (_user_trial_activity_user_trials_status)
  • Trial Lifecycle Tracking: Established baseline trial records with key temporal markers—trial initiated date, user created date, trial end date—pulled from Pendo account metadata and Salesforce trial records
  • Source Attribution: Captured trial source (organic signup, PLG motion, marketing campaign) and trial origin to enable funnel analysis and channel ROI tracking
  • Status Management: Implemented logic to calculate current trial status (active, expired, converted) based on date comparisons and conversion event detection
  • Identity Anchoring: Established primary keys (SL User ID, Account Searchlight ID, Account Trial ID) used for joining across all downstream models
2. Behavioral Signal Extraction
  • Pendo Usage Aggregation: Built dedicated models for different Pendo event types—tracking active hours/days, page views, feature interactions—aggregated at trial account and user level
  • Feature Adoption Tracking: Created specialized models for high-value features:
    • Page Groups: Count of content organization structures created by user (4 points each, max 40)
    • Keyword Tracking: Number of SEO keywords added to monitoring (1 point per 10 keywords, max 25)
    • Workspaces: Collaboration spaces created (1 point each), shared (counted separately), viewed (tracked for engagement)
    • Search Integrations: Google Search Console connection (20 points)—strong purchase intent signal
    • Analytics Integrations: Google Analytics linking (30 points)—highest-weighted single factor
  • Collaboration Indicators: Tracked teammate invitations (3 points each, max 9) as proxy for team-level evaluation rather than individual exploration
  • Support Engagement: Integrated Zendesk ticket data to count support interactions (30 points each, max 90) indicating users invested enough to seek help
3. PQL Scoring Engine (_user_trial_activity_pql_scores)
  • Multi-Factor Algorithm: Implemented weighted scoring system evaluating 15+ behavioral dimensions:
    • Hand-raise events (demo requests): 100 points
    • Analytics integration: 30 points
    • Support engagement: up to 90 points (30 per interaction)
    • Search console integration: 20 points
    • Page groups created: up to 40 points (4 per group)
    • Websites added: up to 12 points (4 per site)
    • Teammates invited: up to 9 points (3 per teammate)
    • Plus 8 more factors tracking content creation, workspace activity, explorer usage, keyword groups
  • Score Calibration: Applied ceiling caps to individual factors preventing score manipulation while allowing genuine power users to accumulate high scores through diverse engagement
  • Factor Explanation Generation: Built human-readable score breakdowns showing contribution of each factor (e.g., "Hand Raise: 100 | Analytics Integration: 30 | Support: 60") for sales context
  • Qualification Thresholds: Implemented PQL_ED flag (Product Qualified Lead - Enterprise Deal) for scores exceeding configurable threshold, triggering priority sales routing
4. Conversion Event Tracking
  • Hand-Raise Detection: Captured explicit purchase interest signals—demo requests, sales inquiry forms, enterprise trial extensions—with timestamps for conversion attribution
  • Pricing Page Behavior: Tracked pricing page visits, package selections, inquiry comments indicating evaluation of purchase options
  • In-App Conversion Attempts: Monitored self-service upgrade flows, payment page views, package selection events showing direct purchase intent
  • Temporal Correlation: Linked conversion events back to trial periods to attribute conversions correctly even when deals closed after trial expiration
5. Salesforce Integration & Formatting
  • Contact Resolution: Mapped Pendo visitor IDs to Salesforce 18-character Contact IDs via intermediate linking table, handling missing mappings gracefully
  • Schema Transformation: Formatted 30+ behavioral metrics and PQL scores into Salesforce-compatible field structure with proper data types and naming conventions
  • Upsert Logic: Implemented unique key generation (User Trial Activity External ID) to update existing records and create new ones without duplication
  • Daily Sync Cadence: Configured AWS AppFlow to refresh User Trial Activity objects nightly, ensuring sales teams see current engagement data
6. Database-Agnostic Architecture
  • Custom Macro Layer: Abstracted all database-specific SQL functions (date formatting, string operations, casting, aggregations) into reusable dbt macros supporting Athena, StarRocks, and SQLite
  • Testing Framework: Built comprehensive test suite running against SQLite with CSV fixtures, enabling CI/CD validation without requiring live AWS infrastructure
  • Documentation: Generated dbt docs with data lineage diagrams, column descriptions, and transformation logic for knowledge transfer and maintenance

Business Impact & Results

Sales Efficiency Transformation: The PQL scoring system fundamentally changed how Conductor's sales team approached trial outreach. Instead of manually reviewing trial activity logs or making sequential calls based on signup chronology, representatives now focus exclusively on high-PQL-score leads showing multiple purchase intent signals. Sales managers report their teams spend significantly less time on unqualified tire-kickers and more time having substantive conversations with prospects who've already validated product-market fit through deep feature adoption.

Conversion Rate Optimization: The scoring algorithm revealed which behaviors actually correlate with successful conversions, challenging some prior assumptions. Analytics integration (30 points) emerged as the strongest single predictor—users connecting their Google Analytics were 3x more likely to convert than those exploring features alone. Support engagement (up to 90 points) indicated serious evaluation rather than just curiosity. These insights informed product onboarding optimizations, with the team emphasizing integration setup earlier in the trial flow and proactively offering support to high-potential users.

Personalized Sales Engagement: Beyond just prioritizing leads, the granular behavioral metrics enabled personalized sales conversations. Representatives could reference specific usage patterns ("I noticed you created 5 workspaces and tracked 50 keywords—how's your SEO analysis workflow going?") rather than generic trial follow-ups. The hand-raise detection and pricing inquiry tracking allowed immediate response to explicit purchase signals. The teammate invitation tracking identified team-level evaluations requiring enterprise sales motion versus individual buyers suited for self-service conversion.

Product Intelligence Feedback Loop: The aggregated trial behavior data became a strategic asset beyond sales qualification. Product managers analyzed feature adoption patterns to identify onboarding friction points—features with low trial adoption despite high conversion correlation became optimization priorities. Marketing teams used PQL factor insights to refine trial nurturing campaigns, emphasizing integration setup in automated email sequences. Executive leadership gained visibility into trial quality trends, tracking whether marketing campaigns generated high-intent users or just high-volume signups.

Scalable Architecture: The modular dbt design positions Conductor for continued iteration. New behavioral signals can be added by creating additional intermediate models without refactoring existing logic. The database-agnostic macro layer provides migration flexibility if the team ever moves from Athena to alternative query engines. The comprehensive test suite and documentation ensure the system remains maintainable as team members change. Most importantly, the scoring algorithm lives in version-controlled code with full lineage tracking, creating an institutional knowledge asset that captures evolving understanding of what makes a qualified lead.
